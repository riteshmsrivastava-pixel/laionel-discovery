Discovery workspace: quick guide

For Monica. Our shared tracker for the MIT Sloan research study on how banks and insurers govern AI agents.

1Sign in

Open riteshmsrivastava-pixel.github.io/laionel-discovery and sign in with monica31@mit.edu and the password you set. You stay signed in on that browser. Sign out is at the bottom of the left menu.

2It saves by itself

Every change saves automatically and shows up for Ritesh straight away, and his for you. There is no save-all button and nothing to refresh. Only the two of us can see the data.

3What each tab is for

PlanThe four-week plan, counts, and the week-by-week task list. Tick a task when it is done.
CompaniesThe 50 target firms, scored 0 to 8. Click a row to edit, or Add company.
PeopleEveryone we plan to contact, their owner (you or Ritesh) and status. Blank names are role slots to fill from LinkedIn.
MessagesA ready-to-send LinkedIn invite note (under 200 characters) and a longer message for people in your network, for every person. Filter to Monica's list.
ScriptThe interview questions. Run an interview gives a timer and note-taking view during the call.
InterviewsOne record per conversation: notes, quotes, and the H1 to H8 tags.
SynthesisCounts that update from the interviews. Nothing to edit here.
OutreachChannels, LinkedIn search strings and general templates.

4Editing anything

  • Click any row, task or question to open the edit panel on the right.
  • Change the fields, then press Save. Delete is in the same panel.
  • Use the Add buttons at the top of each tab for new items.

5Sending outreach

  • Messages tab, filter Monica's list and Not contacted yet.
  • Fill in [Name] on role slots, tweak the text, then Save edits.
  • Copy note into LinkedIn's "Add a note", or Copy message for people you know.
  • Press Mark as contacted so we never message the same person twice.

6After each interview

Within 24 hours, open Interviews and press Log an interview (or save from Run an interview). Pick the person, paste your notes and best verbatim quotes, and tag each hypothesis: click once for evidence for, twice for against. The person's status changes to Done automatically.

Framing reminder. Every message and call is MIT research: answers stay anonymous, there is nothing to sell, and participants get the findings. Do not propose a solution during the call; ask for dates, systems and real examples.
